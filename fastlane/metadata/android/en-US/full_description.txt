<p>Totch turns the notch (display cutout / punch-hole camera) of your phone into a multi-purpose touch button. Tap, double-tap, long-press or swipe right on the notch to trigger an action.</p>

<p><b>Gestures:</b> tap, double tap, long press, swipe down, swipe left, swipe right.</p>

<p><b>Actions:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Back, Home, Recents, Lock screen, Screenshot</li>
<li>Open notification shade or Quick Settings</li>
<li>Toggle flashlight</li>
<li>Media Play/Pause, Next, Previous; Volume up/down; volume panel</li>
<li>Ring / Silent mode and Do Not Disturb</li>
<li>Brightness up/down, auto brightness, auto-rotate</li>
<li>Toggle Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, airplane mode, location</li>
<li>Launch any installed app</li>
<li>Open a system Settings page, send an app's intent, open a contact</li>
<li>Tap an app's Quick Settings tile</li>
<li>One-handed mode</li>
</ul>

<p><b>How it works:</b> turn on the Totch <b>Accessibility</b> service: an invisible area over the notch receives only the touches made there (no screen content is read). <b>Shizuku is optional</b>: with it Totch reads raw touch events with shell privileges instead, and unlocks extra actions (Quick Settings tiles, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth…), one-tap permissions and updates without confirmation. Runs as a foreground service with a persistent notification.</p>

<p><b>About the Accessibility service (optional):</b> Totch uses the Accessibility API only to perform global actions such as Back, Home, Recents, lock screen, screenshot and opening the notification shade, to open apps from the background, and to block touches on the notch with an invisible, empty overlay so that swiping down there does not pull the notification shade. If you turn them on, it also draws Dynamic Island (with its notification ring) and touch effects around the notch, and receives window changes of System UI only, to know when the shade opens. It does not read, collect or transmit any screen content or typed text.</p>
<p><b>About notification access (optional):</b> if you bind Play/Pause, Next or Previous, Totch recommends granting notification access. It is used to find the active media player and send it play/pause/skip commands, and — only if you turn on Dynamic Island — to know which app posted a notification (package, time, flags, importance and color). Totch never reads the title or text of a notification and does not store or transmit anything.</p>

<p><b>Privacy:</b> no ads, no trackers, no analytics. All settings stay on your device. The Internet permission is used only to read the latest release on GitHub and download its APK: when you tap <i>Check for updates</i>, or by the automatic checks you can turn on (off by default).</p>

<p>Requires Android 12 or newer and a phone with a top display cutout. Gestures can optionally keep working in landscape.</p>
